This project tells a clear story about support ticket management, company responsiveness, issue types, and resolution efficiency within a given time window. 

In 2024, Foufani Technologies emerged as the top company by ticket volume and resolution, contributing significantly to support ticket traffic. The report covers key metrics such as issue resolution rates, spending by issue type, customer sentiment, and ticket priority — helping stakeholders assess operational efficiency and user experience.

🔍 Key Highlights & Interpretations
🏆 Top Company
Foufani Technologies filed the highest number of tickets (13) and also resolved the most (13), indicating both high demand and strong follow-through.

💰 Total Cost
The overall cost incurred was $2,022, with Software Issues being the most expensive at $800, highlighting a potential area for system optimization or better documentation.

📈 Ticket Trends
Peak activity occurred on 2025-03-13 with 9 tickets. This day was also the most effective resolution date, suggesting strong triaging or resource allocation.

🧩 Most Used Solutions
Common fixes included clearing app cache, force full sync, and resetting sync — showing that many issues may be preventable or addressable via better self-service options.

🎭 Sentiment vs. Priority
Sentiments such as frustrated and urgent were each tied to 23.33% of tickets, implying that nearly half of the issues felt high-stress to users.

⚠️ Priority Breakdown
Critical and High priority tickets were equally high (11 each), suggesting that most reported issues were severe and required immediate attention.

🏢 Top Companies by Ticket Volume
Foufani Technologies (13), Hisense Tech (10), and Atech Programs (7) made up the bulk of reported tickets, possibly indicating usage volume or system dependencies.

💼 Spending vs. Issue Category
Highest spend areas:

Software Issues: $800

Account Setup: $498

Network Connectivity: $414
These may reflect areas needing more robust infrastructure or user education.

✅ Resolution Rate by Company
All three companies shown have solid resolution rates:

Foufani Technologies: 13

Hisense Tech: 10

Atech Programs: 7
This shows effective handling of support issues across the board.

**Final Recommendations**

1. Since Software Installation Failures, Account Sync Bugs, and Network Connectivity Issues contribute most to costs, efforts should focus on reducing these problems through better testing and preemptive troubleshooting.
2. Frequent solutions (clearing cache, full sync, resetting tokens) suggest recurring issues. Work on permanent fixes rather than temporary workarounds.
3. Foufanni Technologies has the highest volume of tickets, indicating they need additional support. Assign dedicated support resources to this client or train their teams on self-resolution strategies.
4. Since March 13 was the most effective resolution day, analyze what worked well and replicate those strategies on other high-ticket days.
5. Given the high number of frustrated and urgent tickets, consider implementing: Faster response times, proactive issue detection, better documentation and user guides to minimize user confusion.
6. Since March 13 saw the highest number of tickets, investigate whether: A system update was released around that date, External factors (e.g., high traffic, maintenance) contributed to the spike.
7. Adjust system updates or customer support staffing accordingly.

🧠 **Conclusion**
The dashboard gives a well-rounded view of historical support activity for Jeroid.NG. It tells a story of effective issue resolution, with areas like software performance and user frustration highlighting opportunities for future system or process improvements.
